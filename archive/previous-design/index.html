Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work without internet?

CropBill AI requires an internet connection to scan and read bills initially, but your records are saved on your device for offline viewing anytime.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Your bills and financial data are encrypted and private. We never share your data with seed companies, dealers, or advertisers.

Which languages are supported?

Currently, the app supports English and Hindi, with more regional languages coming soon.
